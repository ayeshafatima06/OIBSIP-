The route every lot travels

Same five stages, every time — so the only variable left is the farm itself.

Harvest

Hand-picked

Only ripe cherry, picked in passes over 6–8 weeks.

Process

Washed or natural

Method chosen per lot to suit the cherry and climate.

Dry

Raised beds

Slow-dried over 2–3 weeks, turned by hand daily.

Roast

Small batch

12kg batches, profiled per origin, cupped before release.

Ship

Within 48hrs

Sealed and sent within two days of the roast date.
